Methodology

  • Source: TMDB /discover, filtered to Netflix (with_watch_providers=8, watch_region=US, flatrate). First-party API, not a third-party CSV.
  • Method: paginated discover across movies + series, deduplicated by TMDB id → 7,498 unique titles.
  • Reproducible: re-run fetch_netflix_catalog.py with any TMDB key and you get this dataset. Refreshed weekly via GitHub Actions.
  • Source separation: these charts use the catalog layer only (what Netflix carries). A separate live "trending/top-rated" layer measures demand — the two are never joined.
  • Ratings: TMDB community vote averages; rating charts filter to titles with ≥10 votes to cut noise.
  • Limitation: reflects TMDB's view of Netflix-US availability (sourced via JustWatch), at a point in time — not Netflix's internal catalog.
  • Tools: Python · pandas · Plotly. Zero synthetic records.

Genre mix

Drama (3,235) and Comedy (2,484) anchor the library, with Documentary (1,182) a strong third — a signature of Netflix's originals-era investment in unscripted and prestige nonfiction. Genres are multi-label (a title can count in several), so totals exceed the title count.

Supply shape

The catalog is 4,125 films and 3,373 series (55% / 45%) — film-tilted in raw count, but the series half carries the quality edge (see ratings below).

When the catalog was made

90% of dated titles are from 2015 or later — the catalog is overwhelmingly a product of the streaming-originals era, with a long tail back to 1962.

How good is it

Ratings cluster between 6 and 8 with a mean of 6.82/10 across 5,951 rated titles — a solidly-curated library, not a volume dump. 43% sit at 7.0 or higher.

Popular ≠ acclaimed

Rating and popularity correlate only weakly (r = 0.28) — what's watched isn't what's rated highest. The high-rating / high-popularity quadrant (upper right) is the natural shortlist for promotion and acquisition lookalikes.

Series out-rate film

Series average 7.2 vs 6.5 for films — a 0.7-point edge. Netflix's heavy series investment shows up as measurably higher audience scores.

A global library

51% of the catalog is non-English by original language — Netflix-US is a genuinely international service, not a domestic one with subtitles bolted on.
